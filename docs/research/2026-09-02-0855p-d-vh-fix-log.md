## v1.1 — 2026-09-02

W9.5 fix batch: Status header v1.1 (supersedes v1.0 baseline); TeardownProofEnvelope BLS12-381 aggregate signature scheme requires proof-of-possession (PoP) at witness-set registration — `pop = bls_sign(sk, "OCTO/1/BLS/POP/v1" || pk_g2)` submitted alongside the G2 public key; recipients reject registration without valid PoP. PoP mitigates rogue-key attack (Boneh-Lynn-Shacham 2003 §3) by binding the private-key holder to the public key at registration time, preventing an attacker from registering a public key whose discrete log they do not know. PoP storage: cached alongside witness set entry; PoP verification cost: one pairing check per registration (one-time); per RFC-0853 §Cryptographic Primitives §Proof-of-possession requirement. `SubGroupLabel::new` UTS-39 confusable-script reject set enumerated explicitly (Cyrillic U+0400–U+04FF, Greek U+0370–U+03FF, Armenian U+0530–U+058F, Hebrew U+0590–U+05FF, Arabic U+0600–U+06FF, Cherokee U+13A0–U+13FF) — prior wording "Cyrillic/Greek/Arabic/etc." was descriptive not exhaustive; enumeration closes the per-script codepoint gaps.
