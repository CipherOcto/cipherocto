### Reviewer Lenses (8 distinct)

1. **AC verifiability** — every acceptance criterion maps to a concrete test vector or executable check
2. **Cross-RFC atomicity** — RFC-0205 + RFC-0206 land together or not at all (rule 5)
3. **Fabricated mechanism detection** — no ad-hoc mechanisms smuggled into the RFC body to satisfy ACs
4. **Scope creep** — RFC body stays within scope declared in §Scope header
5. **Dependency correctness** — declared deps match the substrate's actual public API
6. **Threat-model completeness** — every promoted layer A invariant has a threat enumeration
7. **Mission-scope coherence** — sibling missions reference consistent SHAs and names
8. **TV-execution ordering** — TV IDs follow the AC ordering; no AC without a TV

### Activities

- Dispatch all 8 reviewers in parallel (no board chair; methodology disputes escalate per BLUEPRINT.md §Adversarial Review Process)
- Each reviewer produces ≥2 reports (one per RFC) using the reviewer-prompt template
- Aggregate findings into `docs/audits/rfc-0205-0206-r10-findings-2026-08-20.md` with severity tally per RFC

- Zero unresolved CRITICAL findings required for closure (see Acceptance Criterion gates)

## Acceptance Criterion

- ≥16 reviewer reports dispatched (8 reviewers × ≥2 reports each: one per RFC) with structured findings (severity, fix, lens tag, RFC reference)
- Aggregate doc at `docs/audits/rfc-0205-0206-r10-findings-2026-08-20.md` with severity tally per RFC (CRIT/HIGH/MED/LOW counts)
- **CRITICAL gate** (split into two branches — both must hold at closure):
  - **(a)** ZERO CRITICAL findings across all 16 reports, OR
  - **(b)** every CRITICAL finding has BOTH: (i) a corresponding follow-on mission filed at `missions/open/` with `depends_on: [RFC-0205]` or `[RFC-0206]`, AND (ii) an explicit resolution path committed on `next` (commit SHA referenced in the follow-on mission body) — no CRITICAL finding may remain unresolved at closure
- **HIGH gate**: HIGH findings either resolved before closure OR tracked in follow-on missions at `missions/open/`. Wholesale rewrite trigger ties to **cross-cutting defect density** (per R1 baseline): if ≥4 distinct defect _classes_ re-emerge across reviewers (e.g., "phantom cross-ref", "fabricated mechanism", "scope creep", "missing TV" — i.e., pattern, not per-instance), then a v2.0 wholesale rewrite of the affected RFC is mandatory regardless of absolute HIGH tally. Absolute HIGH cap of 10 remains as a backstop (whichever trigger fires first wins). No follow-on backlog tolerance past 10 HIGH or ≥4 re-emerging classes.
- **MED/LOW**: tracked in follow-on backlog; no closure gate beyond the tracker
- RFC body byte-equal at SHA `54d8dd07` (per Condition 4): reviewer read from `git show 54d8dd07:rfcs/accepted/storage/0205-stoolap-fork-stability.md` and `:0206-octo-storage-split.md`

### SHA Pinning Note

The SHA `54d8dd07` is pinned to the **RFC body** (the bytes under `rfcs/accepted/storage/`), NOT to this mission file. Mission files are mutable by design (they accumulate review rounds, status updates, version increments); pinning a SHA to a mission file would invalidate the pin on every commit. The gate is satisfied when `git show 54d8dd07:rfcs/accepted/storage/0205-stoolap-fork-stability.md` and `:0206-octo-storage-split.md` are byte-equal to the current RFC body — regardless of any intervening mission-file edits.
