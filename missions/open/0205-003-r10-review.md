### SHA Pinning Note

The SHA `54d8dd07` is pinned to the **RFC body** (the bytes under `rfcs/accepted/storage/`), NOT to this mission file. Mission files are mutable by design (they accumulate review rounds, status updates, version increments); pinning a SHA to a mission file would invalidate the pin on every commit. The gate is satisfied when `git show 54d8dd07:rfcs/accepted/storage/0205-stoolap-fork-stability.md` and `:0206-octo-storage-split.md` are byte-equal to the current RFC body — regardless of any intervening mission-file edits.
