## §2 Governance Quorum Carryover

Closed by parent RFC-0968 §28 amendment 26 (Session 1 I-5): `GOVERNANCE_QUORUM = 3` constant + `GovernanceProof.governance_set_hash: [u8; 32]` field. Implementation MUST verify the active-set digest at the snapshot where `governance_pubkey` is active. Single-key-compromise attack closed.

## §3 Reserved Codepoint Range — Parent §13 vs Substrate Drift

**Parent RFC-0968 §13 table** documents discriminants `0x01..=0x29`. Parent line 2641, verbatim: "Round 10 M1: the table is monotonic 0x01..=0x29; `Storage(_)` sits at 0x21 between `SuspensionAuthInvalid` (0x20) and `RecorderLifecycleCorrupted` (0x22). 0x2A..=0xFF are reserved for future variants." The monotonic claim refers to the parent §13 table ordering, not the substrate source order.

**Substrate `crates/octo-reputation/src/error.rs`** implements 12 codepoints in the range `0x2A..=0x3A` per substrate source order: `0x2A, 0x2B, 0x2C, 0x2D, 0x2E, 0x2F, 0x30, 0x31, 0x32, 0x33, 0x34, 0x3A` (verified via `grep "= 0x[2-3][0-9A-F]" crates/octo-reputation/src/error.rs`). All 12 are substrate drift from parent §13 (parent documents only `0x01..=0x29` + reserves `0x2A..=0xFF`). A2 ratifies only `0x34` (amendment 28); the other 11 are logged for traceability only:

| Codepoint     | Variant                                                                               | Source line    | A2 disposition                  |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| `0x2A..=0x32` | 9 substrate variants (incl. `0x2E = RotationProvenanceMissingTombstoned` at line 193) | various        | logged; not ratified            |

Substrate doc-comment internally describes two contradicting assigned-range ceilings: `error.rs:4` says `0x01..=0x3A` matches "post-amendment RFC table"; `error.rs:11-12` says real-variant ceiling is `0x39` "Per amendment 82". The "amendment 82" reference is **substrate-local** (not in parent RFC §28 which runs rows 1-27) and is recorded here for traceability only — A2 does NOT propagate this as an RFC amendment number. Substrate `is_reserved()` (function defined at lines 284-286 with doc-comment at line 283; called at line 425 in `controller_id_missing_round_trips_to_0x34` and lines 443-446 in `reserved_range_is_3b_to_ff`) treats `0x3B..=0xFF` as reserved, returning `false` for `0x3A`, `0x34`, `0x32`.

Of the 17 codepoints `0x2A..=0x3A`, substrate implements 12 (`0x2A..=0x34` plus `0x3A`); the remaining 5 (`0x35..=0x39`) are reserved by parent §13 but unassigned by both parent and substrate.
